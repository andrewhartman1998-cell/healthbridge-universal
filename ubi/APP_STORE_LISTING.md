## Description

UBI Global is the world's first comprehensive Universal Basic Income platform — free, multilingual, and built for every human on Earth.

Inspired by Andrew Yang's Freedom Dividend vision, UBI Global brings together education, policy research, real-world pilot data, an impact calculator, and advocacy tools — in 50 languages.

ABOUT UBI
Universal Basic Income is a regular, unconditional cash payment to every citizen — regardless of income, employment, or status. No means-testing. No bureaucracy. Just money in your pocket, every month.

It has been advocated by Martin Luther King Jr., Milton Friedman, Richard Nixon, and Andrew Yang. It is working today in Alaska, Iran, Kenya, and across Europe.
