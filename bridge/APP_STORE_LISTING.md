## DESCRIPTION (4000 chars max)

Bridge to Stability is a free platform connecting people experiencing homelessness and members of underserved communities to stable jobs, safe housing, and critical support resources — with zero barriers to access.

**No fees. No judgment. No background check required.**

---

FIND WORK THAT WELCOMES YOU
Browse jobs specifically filtered for real-world barriers:
• Jobs that accept applicants with no fixed address
• Jobs that accept applicants without a government ID
• Same-day cash pay options
• Day labor, part-time, full-time, and remote opportunities
• Every employer has been vetted for genuine inclusivity

FIND SAFE, STABLE HOUSING
Search housing options filtered for what you actually need:
• Emergency shelters with immediate availability
• Transitional housing programs (30 days to 24 months)
• Sober living environments with peer support
• Tiny homes and shared housing at low or no cost
• Filters for: families welcome, pets OK, LGBTQ+ affirming, wheelchair accessible

ACCESS CRITICAL SUPPORT RESOURCES
One place for everything else you need:
• Food banks and meal programs (Feeding America network)
• Free phone service (FCC Lifeline program)
• Free job training (US Job Corps — 100+ locations)
• ID recovery assistance (birth certificates, state ID, Social Security)
• Mental health support and crisis counseling
• Legal aid and immigration services
• 211 social services hotline integration

BUILD YOUR PROFILE
Create a private profile to personalize your results:
• Tell us what you're looking for (work, housing, or both)
• Share your skills and work preferences
• Note practical factors like transportation access
• Your data is never sold or shared — ever

EMERGENCY HOTLINES — ALWAYS VISIBLE
• National Homelessness Hotline: 1-800-HOMELESS
• Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
• 211 Social Services: Dial 2-1-1

---

BUILT ON A SIMPLE BELIEF:
Stability is not a privilege. Every nonviolent person deserves access to the tools that make a stable life possible. Bridge to Stability was built to be that bridge.

This app is and will always be completely free.
