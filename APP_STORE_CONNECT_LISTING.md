## APP STORE INFORMATION

### Description (paste exactly — 3,847 chars)
```
HealthBridge Universal — Connecting Communities to Care.

Managing your health shouldn't be complicated. HealthBridge Universal brings patients, doctors, and healthcare administrators together in one secure, beautifully designed platform — so the focus stays where it belongs: on care.

──────────────────────────
FOR PATIENTS
──────────────────────────

Your health information, always at your fingertips.

• Appointment Tracking — See all your upcoming and past appointments at a glance. Know exactly when, where, and with whom your next visit is.

• Medical Records — Access your complete health history in one place. Lab results, prescriptions, imaging reports, visit summaries — organized and easy to read.

• Smart Notifications — Never miss an appointment or important health update. Receive real-time reminders and alerts directly from your care team.

• Health Overview — Your blood type, allergies, chronic conditions, and insurance information — always current, always accessible.

──────────────────────────
FOR DOCTORS
──────────────────────────

Everything you need to deliver exceptional care.

• Daily Schedule — Your appointments for today and the week ahead, clearly organized. Complete visits, add notes, and stay on top of your workload.

• Patient Records — View full patient histories, allergies, chronic conditions, and prior records before every appointment. Add clinical notes, prescriptions, and lab results instantly.

• Direct Patient Communication — Notify patients of new records, test results, or follow-up instructions with one tap.

• At-a-Glance Patient Cards — Critical health flags surfaced automatically so nothing gets missed.

──────────────────────────
FOR ADMINISTRATORS
──────────────────────────

Full visibility and control over your healthcare operation.

• Patient Management — Add, search, and manage your entire patient roster with detailed profiles.

• Appointment Scheduling — Book and manage appointments across your entire team. Update statuses and track completion in real time.

• Staff Directory — Manage your medical team, track specialties and departments, and monitor availability.

• Operations Dashboard — A live overview of today's schedule, pending appointments, active patients, and staff — all in one view.

──────────────────────────
BUILT FOR UNDERSERVED COMMUNITIES
──────────────────────────

HealthBridge Universal was designed with equity at its core. Whether you're running a community health clinic, a rural practice, or a growing medical organization, HealthBridge scales to fit your needs.

──────────────────────────
PRIVACY & SECURITY
──────────────────────────

Your health data is sacred. HealthBridge Universal is built with security as a foundation.

• Role-based access control — each user sees only what they're authorized to see
• End-to-end encrypted data transmission (TLS 1.2+)
• Designed with HIPAA best practices
• No third-party data selling, ever

HealthBridge Universal — because access to great healthcare is a right, not a privilege.
```
