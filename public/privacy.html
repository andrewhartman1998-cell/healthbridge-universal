Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of HealthBridge Universal, including:

  • Patients — individuals using the app to manage their own health information
  • Doctors & Clinicians — licensed healthcare providers delivering care through the platform
  • Administrators — healthcare organization staff managing the platform and patient records

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information — name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
  • Health Information — medical records, appointment history, diagnoses, allergies, chronic conditions, prescriptions, lab results, imaging reports, blood type
  • Insurance Information — insurance provider name and member ID
  • Clinical Notes — notes, summaries, and content added by your care team

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data — features accessed, pages viewed, actions within the app
  • Device Information — device type, operating system, app version
  • Log Data — access timestamps and error logs (no health data in logs)
✅
We do NOT collect: Precise geolocation · Contacts or address book · Browsing history outside the app · Financial or payment information · Biometric data (fingerprint, face scan) · Advertising identifiers

How We Share Your Information

Within Your Care Team

Your health information is shared within the app only with healthcare providers and administrators at your organization who are authorized to access it as part of your care.

Service Providers

We work with trusted technology partners who help us operate the platform (hosting, infrastructure, security). All partners are contractually bound to protect your data and may not use it for any other purpose.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HealthBridge, our users, or the public.

🛡️
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information to any third party. Ever.
5

HIPAA Compliance

HealthBridge Universal is designed with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) best practices. We implement all three required safeguard categories:

  • Administrative Safeguards — access controls, audit logs, workforce access policies
  • Physical Safeguards — secure data center infrastructure with access restrictions
  • Technical Safeguards — TLS 1.2+ encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access, session timeouts

Healthcare organizations may need to execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for full HIPAA compliance. Contact hipaa@healthbridge.app to request one.

6

Data Security

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Role-based access control — users only see data they're authorized to access
  • Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure authentication required for every session

If you suspect unauthorized access, contact us immediately at security@healthbridge.app.

7

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, as necessary to provide the Service, or as required by applicable law. Medical records may be subject to statutory retention requirements.

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

Children's Privacy

HealthBridge Universal is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, contact us immediately at privacy@healthbridge.app and we will delete it promptly.

10

Push Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications for appointment reminders, new medical records, and important health updates from your care team. You can disable push notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.
