## Description (4,000 characters max)

GrantBridge makes it easy for any nonprofit — regardless of size or experience — to find grants, simplify the application process, and secure the funding they need to change the world.

**There are 1.8 million nonprofits in the US alone. 72% of small nonprofits never apply for grants — not because the money isn't there, but because the process is too complex. GrantBridge fixes that.**

Free. Always.

---

🔍 FIND GRANTS
Search federal, state, foundation, and corporate grants filtered by:
• Your cause area (Health, Education, Housing, Food, Tech, and more)
• Grant type (Federal / Foundation / Corporate)
• Difficulty level (Low to Very High)
• Award amount
Includes detailed eligibility requirements, deadlines, competition level, and direct links to apply.

📝 GRANT APPLICATION WIZARD
A step-by-step guided wizard that walks you through every section of a grant proposal:
1. Organization Information
2. Mission & Impact
3. Project Description
4. Budget + Budget Narrative
5. Evaluation Plan
6. Review & Export

Download a complete, formatted application draft as a text file — ready to paste into any grant portal. Cuts grant writing time by 60%.

📊 APPLICATION TRACKER
Track every grant in one place:
• Application status (Researching, Writing, Submitted, Under Review, Awarded, Declined)
• Deadlines with color-coded urgency
• Notes per application
• Summary dashboard: total tracked, submitted, awarded, and total dollars won

📚 GRANT RESOURCES
Free guides and templates including:
• The Grant Writing Formula (proven 5-part structure)
• Budget Template — Grant Ready
• Letter of Intent (LOI) Template
• Top Free Grant Databases
• Free Grant Writing Training & Certifications
