## Description (4000 chars max)

Power back to the people — everywhere on Earth.

VoiceOfThePeople Global is a free, open democratic platform built for every citizen on the planet. Inspired by the mixed government concept championed by Aristotle, Montesquieu, and the Founders of modern democracy — this app restores accountability, transparency, and real political power to the people.

Available in 50 languages with full RTL support.
