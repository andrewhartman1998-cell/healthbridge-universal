## Description

Every citizen deserves to know what government benefits they're entitled to — and actually be able to access them.

GovBridge Global connects people in every country to the disability assistance, housing support, food programs, healthcare subsidies, unemployment insurance, veterans benefits, and emergency relief their governments provide — in plain language, in 50 languages.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔍 BENEFITS FINDER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Search 2,400+ government assistance programs across 40+ countries. Filter by disability, housing, food, healthcare, unemployment, veterans, family support, education, and senior care. Every listing includes eligibility requirements, benefit amounts, agency contacts, and direct application links.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📋 ELIGIBILITY CHECKER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Answer 8 simple questions about your situation. GovBridge instantly matches you to the programs you're most likely to qualify for — with priority ratings (URGENT, HIGH, MEDIUM) and benefit amounts. Available for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and more.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
📝 APPLICATION ASSISTANT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Step-by-step application guides for major benefit programs: SSDI, SSI, SNAP, Section 8, PIP, Universal Credit, NDIS, CPP-D, and more. Document checklists, application steps, and insider tips for each program. No guesswork.

♿ DISABILITY ACCESS CENTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Dedicated deep-dive into disability programs worldwide: SSDI, SSI, VA Disability (US), PIP, DLA (UK), CPP-D (Canada), DSP, NDIS (Australia), and international equivalents. Expandable step-by-step application guidance with tips from real applicants.
