# GovBridge Global — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 4, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 4, 2026

## Overview
GovBridge Global ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- Personal identity information (name, email, SSN, date of birth)
- Location data
- Medical or disability information
- Financial information
- Device identifiers beyond standard analytics

## How the App Works
All eligibility check answers and benefit searches are processed locally on your device. Nothing you enter is transmitted to external servers or stored in any database.

## Language Preferences
Your selected language is stored locally in your device's browser storage (localStorage) only. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Links
GovBridge links to official government websites (SSA.gov, GOV.UK, NDIS.gov.au, etc.). We have no control over the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their policies independently.

## Children's Privacy
This app is rated 4+ and contains no features that collect data from children.
