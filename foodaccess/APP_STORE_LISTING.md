## Description (4,000 characters max)

FoodAccess Global connects every person on Earth to free and affordable healthy food — food banks, community gardens, meal programs, nutrition guides, and global food aid organizations.

**733 million people face hunger worldwide. 3.1 billion cannot afford a healthy diet. That changes today.**

FoodAccess Global is 100% free. No ads. No subscriptions. No barriers.

---

🔍 FIND FOOD NEAR YOU
Search thousands of food banks, soup kitchens, community fridges, meal programs, and SNAP/WIC-accepting retailers. Filter by:
• No ID required
• Walk-in welcome
• Serves children
• Serves seniors
• Emergency food programs

🌿 COMMUNITY GARDENS
Find local growing spaces, seed libraries, and urban farms. Grow your own food, join a community plot, or borrow seeds — all free.

🍎 NUTRITION GUIDANCE
Free, plain-language nutrition guides for every situation:
• Eating healthy on $5 a day
• Nutrition for babies and children
• Nutrition for older adults
• Global nutrition facts
• Vitamins and supplement guide

🌍 GLOBAL FOOD MAP
An interactive map of food resources across every region of Earth — North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Middle East, Oceania, and Global Programs. From rural villages to urban centers — help is always nearby.

🚨 EMERGENCY HOTLINES ALWAYS VISIBLE
• US National Hunger Hotline: 1-866-3-HUNGRY
• SNAP Benefits: 1-800-221-5689
• WFP Global: wfp.org

WHO THIS IS FOR:
• Anyone facing food insecurity
• Families stretching a tight budget
• Parents wanting better nutrition for their children
• Seniors seeking meal delivery
• Community organizations serving vulnerable populations
• Anyone who wants to help end global hunger

Our belief: Hunger is not a scarcity problem — it's a distribution and access problem. Every person on Earth deserves access to safe, nutritious food. FoodAccess Global exists to close that gap.
