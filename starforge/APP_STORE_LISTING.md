## Description

The stars are not reserved for governments or billionaires. They belong to all of us.

StarForge is the world's first open spacecraft design platform — built for scientists, engineers, students, and visionaries worldwide. Design real spacecraft, plan deep space missions, run physics simulations, and collaborate with researchers across the globe.

Available in 50 languages with full RTL support.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🛸 SHIP DESIGNER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Build spacecraft module by module from a library of 18 real engineering components. Choose nose cones, crew habitats, payload bays, solar arrays, and heat radiators. Select propulsion systems: chemical rockets, ion drives, nuclear thermal engines, fusion drives, solar sails, and experimental EM drives. Add onboard systems: LiDAR, deep space antennas, AI flight computers, life support, radiation shielding, and medical bays.

Get real-time engineering data for every design: thrust-to-weight ratio, specific impulse (Isp), and estimated delta-v.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🪐 MISSION PLANNER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Plan missions to 8 destinations: Low Earth Orbit, the Moon, Mars, the Asteroid Belt, Europa, Titan, Pluto, and Proxima Centauri b. Get delta-v requirements, mission duration estimates, crew configurations, and launch window planning tools for each target.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
⚡ SYSTEMS LAB
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Run 6 engineering simulations: Thrust & Trajectory analysis, Thermal modeling for re-entry and deep space, Power budget calculations, Life support resource modeling, Deep space communications link budgets, and Radiation exposure assessments. Input your parameters and get instant AI-powered recommendations.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🌐 GLOBAL COLLABORATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Join active research teams or create your own. Connect with 12,400+ scientists and engineers across 47 active projects — from solar sail propulsion to crewed Mars habitats to laser sail interstellar probes.
