# HealthBridge Universal — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 30, 2026

---

## Introduction

HealthBridge Universal ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the HealthBridge Universal mobile application and web platform (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the terms described here.

---

## 1. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all users of HealthBridge Universal, including:
- **Patients** — individuals using the app to manage their own health information
- **Doctors / Clinicians** — licensed healthcare providers using the app to deliver care
- **Administrators** — healthcare organization staff managing the platform

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information** — name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
- **Health Information** — medical records, appointment history, diagnoses, allergies, chronic conditions, prescriptions, lab results, imaging reports, blood type
- **Insurance Information** — insurance provider name and member ID
- **Communications** — notes, messages, and content you submit within the app

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data** — features accessed, pages viewed, actions taken within the app
- **Device Information** — device type, operating system version, app version
- **Log Data** — access timestamps, error logs (no personally identifiable health data in logs)

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Precise geolocation
- Contacts or address book data
- Browsing history outside the app
- Financial or payment information
- Biometric data (fingerprint, face scan)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate and improve the Service:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|---|---|
| Providing core healthcare management features | Performance of contract |
| Sending appointment reminders and notifications | Legitimate interest / consent |
| Enabling doctors to access patient records for care | Performance of contract |
| Improving app performance and fixing bugs | Legitimate interest |
| Complying with legal obligations | Legal obligation |

We **do not** use your health information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Within Your Care Team
Your health information is shared within the app only with healthcare providers and administrators at your organization who are authorized to access it as part of your care.

### 4.2 Service Providers
We work with trusted technology service providers who help us operate the platform (hosting, infrastructure, security). All service providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may not use it for any purpose other than providing services to us.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of HealthBridge, our users, or the public.

### 4.4 We Do NOT Sell Your Data
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information to any third party. Ever.

---

## 5. HIPAA Compliance

HealthBridge Universal is designed with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) best practices in mind. Where applicable, we implement:

- **Administrative Safeguards** — access controls, workforce training, audit controls
- **Physical Safeguards** — secure data center infrastructure with access restrictions
- **Technical Safeguards** — encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest, role-based access control, automatic session timeouts

Healthcare organizations using HealthBridge may need to execute a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for full HIPAA compliance. Contact us at privacy@healthbridge.app to request a BAA.

---

## 6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

- All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
- Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
- Role-based access control ensures users only see data they're authorized to access
- Regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
- Secure authentication for all sessions

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and to notify us immediately at security@healthbridge.app if you suspect unauthorized access.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:
- Your account is active, or
- Necessary to provide the Service, or
- Required by applicable law (medical records may be subject to retention requirements)

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

### All Users
- **Access** — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction** — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion** — Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Portability** — Request your data in a machine-readable format

### California Residents (CCPA)
- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed (we do not sell it)
- Right to opt out of sale (not applicable — we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

### EU/UK Residents (GDPR)
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to object to processing
- Right to withdraw consent at any time
- Right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@healthbridge.app.
